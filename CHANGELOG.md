# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Jointed death animations for every vanilla mob, chosen by the direction of the killing blow, fall damage, water or lava.
- Special deaths for skeletons, the snow golem, the Warden, creepers, ravagers, blazes, breezes, guardians, nautiluses, armadillos, frogs, slimes and magma cubes.
- Mobs that die in mid-air keep falling and only collapse once they land; bodies in deep water float up to the surface.
- Builds for Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge and Forge, on Minecraft 26.1.x and 26.2-26.3.
- Settings screen through Mod Menu on Fabric and Quilt, built in on NeoForge and Forge.
